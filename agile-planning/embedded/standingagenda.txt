Estimation unit: capture DURATION IN DAYS on each story row. Story Points are DERIVED, not typed — the tracker computes ROUNDUP(Duration / 2) per story and sums them onto the epic, and the column is locked. Keep Durations on 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 days, which map exactly to 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 points; an off-scale duration yields an off-scale point value (7d gives 4, and there is no 4 on the scale).
